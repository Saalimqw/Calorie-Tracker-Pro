**Pro Calorie Tracker & Health Guidance** is a smart, web-based app that helps users understand, plan, and improve their daily calorie intake and overall health habits.

## Overview

This app is designed to guide users through healthy eating and lifestyle choices with a focus on calorie awareness, nutrition, and simple tracking.  It is optimized for mobile-first usage and runs smoothly in modern browsers, making it easy to access from any device.

## Features

- Daily calorie tracking with clear visual feedback on intake versus goals.
- Health guidance focused on better food choices, portion control, and sustainable habits.
- Simple, clean interface tailored for quick logging and checking progress on the go.

## Getting Started

1. Open the app in your browser using the provided URL.
2. Enter your basic details (such as weight goals and activity level, if supported) to personalize guidance.
3. Start logging your meals and reviewing the feedback to align with your calorie and health goals.

## Use Cases

- Individuals who want a lightweight calorie tracker without complex setup.
- Users beginning a fitness or weight management journey and needing simple guidance on daily intake.
- Anyone who prefers a web app experience that works across devices instead of a heavy native app.

## Notes

Remember to treat the app as a general guidance tool and, for medical or specialized dietary needs, consult a healthcare professional or registered dietitian.
